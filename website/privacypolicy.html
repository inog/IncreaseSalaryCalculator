This privacy policy applies to the Android mobile application IncreaseSalaryCalculator (Package: de.ingoreschke.increasesalarycalculator, hereinafter "App"), developed and published by Ingo Reschke.

1. Data Controller

The controller responsible for data processing in accordance with GDPR is:

Ingo Reschke
Email: isc@ingo-reschke.de

2. Local Data Storage

Protecting your privacy is our top priority. The App does not collect or transmit personal identification data such as names, email addresses, or banking details.

For your convenience across sessions, the following settings are stored strictly locally on your device in the App's private sandbox (via Android Jetpack DataStore / SharedPreferences):

  • Last entered salary figures (base and target salary)
  • Last entered percentage increase
  • Selected calculation mode & period (monthly/annual)
  • Preferred currency setting

This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP SDK)

To support free development of the App, we use Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited) to display banner advertisements.

3.1 Data Processing by Google AdMob

Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic data to deliver advertisements, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.

3.2 Consent Management (Google UMP SDK)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom (UK), the App implements Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. This provides a transparent consent prompt adhering to the IAB TCF 2.2 framework, allowing you to choose between personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or manage vendor consents.

For details regarding Google's data processing, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Your Rights Under GDPR / CCPA

You have the right to request information about, update, or erase personal data processed about you. Because calculation data is stored exclusively on your device, you can completely erase all locally stored data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the App.
